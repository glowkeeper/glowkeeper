# Steve Huckle

I make software, games and teaching materials.

My work moves between computing education, creative practice and
research into responsible technology. I’m drawn to things people can play, alter, question
and learn from.

## Selected projects

- **[Quizmo](https://quizmo.fun/)** — ten numerical questions, ten
  seconds each, and layouts that become increasingly unhelpful.

- **[Keep These](https://keepthese.com/)** — a tool for making blackout
  poetry from classic writing. It chooses nothing for you, stores work
  in your browser and keeps every poem connected to its source.
  [Source →](https://github.com/glowkeeper/keepthese)

- **[Feedbacker](https://feedbacker.education/)** — an open-source tool
  for AI-assisted marking and moderation in higher education. Educators
  decide every mark and approve what students receive.
  [Source →](https://github.com/glowkeeper/feedbacker)

- **[My Victorian Retrofit Home](https://retrofit.huckle.studio/)** —
  an explorable 3D model of my Victorian terrace, with stories about
  what I changed, what it cost and what I’ve noticed since.

- **[Rectangle Hunt](https://rectanglehunt.com/)** — an untimed daily
  puzzle about finding rectangles hidden in line drawings.

- **[The Horizontal Front](https://thehorizontalfront.org/)** — a
  satirical arcade game about capitalism and resistance to hierarchy.
  [Source →](https://github.com/glowkeeper/the-horizontal-front)

I also made **[Cream Cheese](https://github.com/glowkeeper/creamcheese)**,
a modern version of Bagels, the old deductive number game.

## Teaching

I design and teach courses in software development, data analysis and
creative computing. My
[Introduction to Building 3D Games in Unity](https://github.com/glowkeeper/IntroToBuilding3DGamesInUnity)
workshop takes you from placing objects in a scene to building a simple
playable game.

## How I work

I like software that is small enough to inspect, useful enough to test
with people and open enough to be challenged. I care about privacy,
accessibility, attribution and understanding the plumbing.

Not everything here is finished. Much of it exists because building
the thing is how I work out what I think.
