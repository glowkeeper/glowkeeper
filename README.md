# Dr Steven Huckle

*Where code, creativity and curiosity meet.*

I’m a computing educator, curriculum designer, software developer and writer exploring the spaces between technology, teaching and creative practice. I build software, games, research artefacts and learning experiences to test ideas in public—and to learn what becomes possible when those forms meet.

## Current focus

My current work connects three strands:

- practical software, games and teaching programmes;
- research into responsible technology, decentralised systems and digital commons; and
- creative practice across writing, games and interactive media.

I’m particularly interested in turning concepts into small, usable systems: artefacts that can be played, taught with, examined, challenged and improved.

## Teaching and curriculum

I design university modules, intensive bootcamps, assessments, workshops and supporting resources for learners and tutors. My current teaching work includes:

- **[Creative Process software development bootcamps](https://github.com/glowkeeper/creative-process-software-bootcamp-aug26)** — eight-week programmes that take new developers from workflows, HTML and CSS through JavaScript, React, testing, deployment and independent projects. An earlier [June 2026 edition](https://github.com/glowkeeper/creative-process-software-bootcamp-jun26) is also public.
- **[Data Visualisation Python Companion](https://github.com/glowkeeper/data-visualisation-python-companion)** — practical material supporting postgraduate Data Visualisation and Intelligence study at the University of Roehampton.
- **Data Analysis and Research Skills (DA&RS)** — curricula, lectures, workshops, assessments and tutor resources that help students reason with evidence and communicate research clearly.
- **Creative computing and games** — Unity-based teaching that connects programming with narrative, visual design, iteration and collaborative development.

My [CV](https://huckle.studio/about/cv) gives the broader context: more than three decades of technical practice, teaching in higher education and career-transition settings, curriculum design, games programming and research.

## Tools and methods

- **TypeScript, JavaScript, React and Next.js** for web applications, games and interfaces
- **Node.js and Payload** for backend and full-stack systems
- **Markdown, Pandoc and LaTeX** for research, papers and presentations
- **Git and GitHub** for versioning, publishing and open collaboration
- **Design Science Research and iterative prototyping** as a methodological backbone
- **Small scripts and transparent tooling** for the glue between systems

I favour simple, composable stacks whose plumbing I can understand. I stay curious about new tools without treating novelty as a goal in itself.

## What I’m learning next

- writing more functionally in TypeScript, React and Next.js;
- using AI in ways that are useful, legible and responsibly governed;
- finding new ways for software, teaching and writing to shape one another;
- designing games as vehicles for ideas as well as play; and
- carrying research prototypes into dependable, accessible systems.
